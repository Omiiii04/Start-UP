# Master Services Agreement (MSA), Terms of Service, Privacy Policy & Intellectual Property Framework

---

# 1. Executive Overview

This document establishes the legal framework governing all engagements between the company and its clients. It defines contractual obligations, payment terms, intellectual property ownership, confidentiality requirements, privacy commitments, acceptable use policies, dispute resolution mechanisms, and statutory compliance requirements.

The objectives of this framework are to:

- Protect the interests of both the company and its clients.
- Establish clear contractual obligations.
- Define ownership of intellectual property.
- Ensure compliance with Indian laws and regulations.
- Support transparent commercial relationships.
- Minimize legal and operational risks.

Unless otherwise agreed in writing, every commercial engagement shall be governed by:

- Master Services Agreement (MSA)
- Statement of Work (SOW)
- Terms of Service (ToS)
- Privacy Policy
- Applicable Indian Laws

---

# 2. Master Services Agreement (MSA)

The Master Services Agreement (MSA) defines the overarching legal relationship between the company and the client.

---

## 2.1 Scope of Services

Services are provided exclusively under individually executed Statements of Work (SOW).

Each SOW defines:

- Project objectives
- Deliverables
- Timeline
- Milestones
- Pricing
- Acceptance criteria
- Support obligations

Where conflicts arise:

```
Statement of Work
        │
        ▼
Master Services Agreement
        │
        ▼
Applicable Indian Law
```

Unless explicitly stated otherwise, the provisions of the MSA prevail.

---

## 2.2 Commercial Terms

- All quotations are issued in Indian Rupees (INR).
- Applicable GST is charged separately unless otherwise stated.
- Pricing follows the milestone structure defined in `Pricing.md`.
- Payment is due according to agreed milestones.
- Late payments beyond **15 calendar days** may attract interest of **1.5% per month**, or the maximum amount permitted under applicable law.

Failure to clear outstanding invoices may result in:

- Suspension of development
- Suspension of support
- Delay in source code transfer
- Suspension of production deployment

---

## 2.3 Confidentiality

Both parties agree to protect confidential information exchanged during the engagement.

Confidential Information includes:

- Source code
- Architecture diagrams
- Technical documentation
- Client business data
- Financial information
- Internal processes
- Credentials
- API documentation

Confidentiality obligations survive for **three (3) years** following termination of the agreement unless otherwise required by law.

---

# 3. Statement of Work (SOW)

Every project shall include a Statement of Work defining:

- Scope
- Deliverables
- Timeline
- Milestones
- Acceptance Criteria
- Assumptions
- Exclusions
- Payment Schedule
- Support Period

Any work outside the approved SOW shall require a formal **Change Order** before implementation.

---

# 4. Intellectual Property Framework

The company distinguishes between **Background IP** and **Foreground IP**.

| Category  | Background IP                               | Foreground IP                                        |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Ownership | Company                                     | Client                                               |
| Examples  | Frameworks, Templates, Libraries, Utilities | Custom Software, Business Logic, UI, Database Design |
| Transfer  | Licensed                                    | Assigned after final payment                         |

---

## 4.1 Background Intellectual Property

Background IP includes:

- Internal frameworks
- Utility libraries
- MLOps templates
- Deployment scripts
- Design systems
- Reusable components
- Automation tools

Ownership remains exclusively with the company.

Clients receive a perpetual, non-exclusive, royalty-free license to use embedded Background IP solely as incorporated within the final deliverable.

---

## 4.2 Foreground Intellectual Property

Foreground IP includes:

- Custom source code
- Client-specific workflows
- Database schemas
- UI designs
- API implementations
- Business logic

Ownership transfers to the client only after:

- Final payment
- Invoice settlement
- Contract completion

---

# 5. Academic Integrity & UGC Compliance

The company strictly complies with the **University Grants Commission (Promotion of Academic Integrity and Prevention of Plagiarism in Higher Educational Institutions) Regulations, 2018**.

The company does **not** provide:

- Ghostwriting
- Assignment completion
- Thesis writing
- Dissertation writing
- Proxy project execution
- Fabricated research

Permitted services include:

- Technical mentorship
- Code review
- Debugging
- Infrastructure setup
- MLOps deployment
- Dataset preprocessing
- Proof-of-concept software
- Architecture consulting

Every academic client must acknowledge the Academic Integrity Declaration before project initiation.

# 6. Terms of Service

By using the company's services, clients agree to:

- Provide accurate information.
- Maintain account security.
- Use delivered software lawfully.
- Respect licensing terms.
- Comply with applicable laws.

The company reserves the right to suspend services in cases involving:

- Fraudulent activity
- Chargebacks
- Abuse
- Unauthorized access attempts
- Illegal usage
- Violation of contractual terms

# 7. Privacy Policy

The company collects only the information necessary to deliver contracted services.

## Data Collected

- Name
- Email
- Phone Number
- Organization
- Billing Information
- GSTIN (if applicable)
- Project Documents
- Communication History
- Payment Records

---

## Data Protection

Security controls include:

- TLS 1.3
- AES-256 Encryption
- Role-Based Access Control
- Secure Cloud Storage
- MFA for Internal Systems

---

## Third-Party Services

The company may use trusted third-party providers, including:

- Razorpay
- Cashfree
- AWS
- GitHub
- SendGrid
- WhatsApp Business API

Payment information is processed directly by PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways and is **never stored** on company servers.

---

# 8. Limitation of Liability

To the maximum extent permitted by law:

- The company is not liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages.
- Liability shall not exceed the total fees paid under the applicable Statement of Work unless otherwise required by law.

The company is not responsible for losses resulting from:

- Client misuse
- Unauthorized modifications
- Third-party software failures
- Force majeure events

---

# 9. Warranty & Support

Unless otherwise specified:

- Software is delivered according to the agreed acceptance criteria.
- A **30-day post-delivery bug-fix warranty** is included.
- Warranty applies only to defects within the agreed project scope.

Warranty excludes:

- New feature requests
- Third-party changes
- Hosting issues outside company infrastructure
- Client modifications

---

# 10. Termination

Either party may terminate the agreement if:

- Material contractual breach occurs.
- Payments remain outstanding.
- Illegal activities are discovered.
- Mutual written agreement is reached.

Upon termination:

- Outstanding invoices remain payable.
- Confidentiality obligations continue.
- Intellectual property transfers follow contractual payment conditions.

---

# 11. Governing Law & Dispute Resolution

This agreement shall be governed by the laws of the **Republic of India**.

Unless otherwise agreed:

- Jurisdiction: **Pune, Maharashtra**
- Governing Courts: Courts located in Pune

The parties shall first attempt to resolve disputes through good-faith negotiation before initiating legal proceedings.

---

# 12. Force Majeure

Neither party shall be liable for delays caused by events beyond reasonable control, including:

- Natural disasters
- Government actions
- War
- Internet outages
- Cloud provider failures
- Pandemics
- Civil unrest

Affected obligations shall resume once the force majeure event has ended.

---

# 13. Amendments

Any modification to:

- MSA
- SOW
- Terms of Service
- Privacy Policy

must be approved in writing by both parties.
