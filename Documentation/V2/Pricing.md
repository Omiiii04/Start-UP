# Pricing.md

## Tiered Pricing Architecture, Rate Cards & Payment Policy Framework

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Pricing Philosophy

The company follows a **value-based, transparent, and standardized pricing model** designed to serve students, researchers, startups, and small-to-medium enterprises (SMEs).

Pricing is based on:

- Project complexity
- Engineering effort
- Infrastructure requirements
- Delivery timeline
- Long-term maintenance requirements

Every quotation follows predefined pricing tiers, milestone-based billing, and statutory GST compliance to eliminate ambiguity and maintain predictable project delivery.

## 4. Milestone Payment Structure

To maintain healthy cash flow and reduce payment risk, all engagements follow milestone-based billing.

---

### Tier 1 Projects

**Projects below ₹15,000**

| Milestone      | Payment |
| -------------- | ------- |
| Project Start  | 50%     |
| Final Delivery | 50%     |

---

### Tier 2 & Tier 3 Projects

**₹15,000 – ₹60,000**

| Milestone             | Payment |
| --------------------- | ------- |
| Advance               | 30%     |
| Staging Demonstration | 40%     |
| Final Delivery        | 30%     |

---

### Tier 4 Projects

**₹60,000+**

| Milestone             | Payment |
| --------------------- | ------- |
| Contract Signing      | 25%     |
| Architecture Approval | 25%     |
| QA & Staging          | 25%     |
| Production Release    | 25%     |

- Source code is not transferred.
- Production deployment is postponed.

## 8. Discount Policy

Discounts are offered selectively to maintain sustainable pricing.

---

### Student Referral Program

- Referral Commission: **5%–10%**
- Applicable on verified commercial leads only.

---

### Academic Research Discount

Eligible academic infrastructure and research support projects may receive a **5% courtesy discount**.

---

### Startup Support

Early-stage startups may receive customized pricing based on:

- Project scope
- Long-term engagement
- Strategic partnership opportunities

---

## 9. Exclusions

Unless explicitly stated in the Statement of Work, quotations do **not** include:

- Domain registration
- Third-party software licenses
- Cloud hosting charges
- SMS or Email gateway fees
- App Store / Play Store fees
- Government registration fees
- Ongoing maintenance beyond the agreed support period

These costs are billed separately where applicable.

---

## 10. Refund Policy

Refund eligibility depends on the project stage.

| Project Stage            | Refund Eligibility       |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------ |
| Before Project Kickoff   | Subject to agreement     |
| After Development Begins | Generally non-refundable |
| Completed Deliverables   | Non-refundable           |

Refund decisions are governed by the executed Master Service Agreement (MSA).
